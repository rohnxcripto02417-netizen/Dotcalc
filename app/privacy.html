Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Dotcalc is developed by Rohan. It does not collect, store on any server, share or sell personal data.

What stays on your device

Your settings (theme, accent color, key size, decimal places, and similar options) and, if you turn on “Remember history”, your calculation history are saved only in your device’s local storage. You can clear them at any time from Settings, or by clearing the app’s data.

What we do not do

The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files, and the only network request it makes is optional: when you open the Currency converter, it downloads public exchange rates from open.er-api.com. No personal data, identifiers or calculations are sent with that request. Every other tool works fully offline.

Permissions

Dotcalc uses the internet only to fetch currency rates, and vibration only for optional key feedback.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.
